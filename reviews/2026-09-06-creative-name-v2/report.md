## What this tests

A new pendant is invented from a customer name, with reusable lettering/hardware/spelling references assigned narrow roles. No finished customer-specific pendant silhouette is supplied. Six fixed names, two repetitions and four nested reference methods share the same construction and photographic instructions. Cases are randomized and frozen before submission. Exact prompts are above images in [the gallery](review.html). [Blind originals](blind/manifest.json), [the rubric](rubric.json), [ledger](ledger.json) and [case manifest](cases.json) retain the evidence.

The first design must preserve name spelling, dots/hamzas, supported components and plausible hardware. A beautiful material rendering cannot override those checks. Every required connection must be visible. Uncertainty fails advancement. Reviews are blinded assistant visual assessments, not a professional calligraphy or manufacturing certification.
