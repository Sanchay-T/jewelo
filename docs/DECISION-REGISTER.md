## ADR acceptance checklist

- decision and scope;
- business and technical drivers;
- current primary-source evidence with access date;
- options and weighted comparison;
- cost/latency/operational implications;
- security, privacy, regional, and vendor-exit implications;
- failure/rollback strategy;
- consequences and review trigger;
- explicit approver.
