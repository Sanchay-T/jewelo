# Independent jewelry image review — 6 September 2026

Scope: all 70 customer catalogue images existing before the new cumulative batch (5 v1 and 65 accepted v2), plus all 12 v3 cumulative images. Every image was directly opened with `view_image`; suspected fine connections were inspected again with enlarged pixel crops. Rejected historical source attempts that were already excluded from the customer catalogue were not reviewed.

Result: 4 definite visual continuity failures, all Arabic v2 Studio masters. The other 66 existing images and all 12 v3 images show no observed broken-jewelry defect at the inspected resolution. This is visual acceptance for illustrative assets, not manufacturing, material, scale or spelling certification.

## Corrected preliminary findings

Heart and Interlocked Fatima i-dots initially appeared detached at full-frame size. Enlarged crops show fine gold necks in the Studio, Close-up and Dark views; the worn views are small and the attachment is less legible. These are readability concerns, not established breakage. The earlier provisional hard-rejection message for these families is withdrawn. Strengthening these necks may improve clarity, but should preserve the design.

The lower rail in `rails-dark.png` also has small visible support joints on enlargement. Accent diamonds show gold mountings when enlarged; no floating stones were confirmed. `natural-studio.png` has relatively soft diamond texture, an image-quality concern rather than a confirmed structural fault.
