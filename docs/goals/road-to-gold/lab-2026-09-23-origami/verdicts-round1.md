No wrong spelling, no extra or missing glyph, no floating stone or mark, no extra ring, and no chain lying beside a ring in any of the six.
The only recurring defect is weight: the strokes are thicker than "refined", worst on the Latin v1 pair.

## Ranking

1. v2-asmaar - correct, crisp, and the only one that already reads as restrained fine jewelry.
2. v2-love - same crisp large-panel construction, letters exact, only slightly bold.
3. v1-asmaar - correct and elegant, a little heavier in the baseline than its v2 twin.
4. v2-asma - clean construction, chunky Latin caps.
5. v1-love - correct but the heaviest of the Latin set.
6. v1-asma - correct letters, but small busy facets and soft creases lose the folded crispness.

## Recommendation

Take **v2 (the freer outline) as the base for the refinement round.** The freer outline cost nothing on spelling - all three v2 names are letter-exact against the stencil - and it bought larger, flatter, crisper panels, sharper creases and visibly slimmer strokes than v1, which is exactly the direction Omran's "the same in a more decent manner" asks for.

Tune exactly one axis next: **stroke slimness.** Slim the letter stems by about 20 percent, from the current slab bar to a 2.0 mm apparent stroke in the 32 x 12 x 2.5 mm footprint, and keep the panel count, panel size and depth as they are in v2. The panels and creases in v2 are already right; only the mass is wrong.
