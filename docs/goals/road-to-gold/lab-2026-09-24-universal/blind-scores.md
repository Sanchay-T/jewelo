# UNIV-1 blind scoring - 48 images

Method: every image opened and read at full size; every joint in doubt re-cropped at 3-4x.
Connectivity was also measured objectively: the pendant was segmented from the background and its connected components counted at erosion 0 (fully separate parts) and erosion 4 px (joints thinner than about 8 px, roughly 0.2 mm at this scale).
A part that only survives as connected at erosion 0 but splits at erosion 4 is scored as a hair-thin joint and fails one piece.

Note on "presentable" for diamond-rails: the brief defines that construction as the name between two rails set with small diamonds.
Only u06, u15, u16 and u24 have stones in the rails; the other twelve rails pieces have plain gold bars, and those are marked presentable fail with the reason "rails carry no stones".
If plain rails are an accepted variant, those twelve flip to presentable pass.
