# Style catalog

Looks Omran sent in the Jewelry AI group.
These are candidate pendant architectures, not the locked six.

Source index: `context/whatsapp/media/index.md`.

## Status labels

- `exploration` — dumped 27 Aug, then told to narrow.
- `positive` — re-sent 29 Aug as wanted.
- `conditional` — re-sent 29 Aug as yes, but must be done much better.
- `asked` — named in text, no dedicated still.
- `spec` — CALEUMS specification sheet; quality bar, not auto-approval of every depicted stone.

Overlaps are real.
Origami, ribbon, and constellation appear as both concept cards and spec sheets.

## Spec-sheet family

S24–S29 set the photography and construction bar:
multi-angle, attachments, stone placement, dimensions, metal, brand presentation.

They overlap origami / ribbon / constellation.
Do not treat every stone journey on a spec sheet as mandatory for every look.

## What this is not

Arabic calligraphy options in the current app (Classic, Minimal, Diwani, Kufi, Signature, Thuluth) are lettering engines.
They are not rows in this catalog.

Two-name connectors (heart, infinity, stacked) are not rows in this catalog.

Studio / On skin / Close up / Dark are camera shots of one piece.
They are not looks.

## Open
